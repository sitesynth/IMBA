# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small internal team (2-5 operators). Context: desktop browser, task-focused workflow — managing users, monitoring payments, reviewing referral partners. Fast scanning over reading; they know the domain.

## Product Purpose

IMBA admin panel: manage users, VPN subscriptions, eSIM orders, payments, referral programs, and promocodes. The admin is the operator's control room — every action has a consequence (block a user, confirm a payout, create a plan). Success means operators find what they need fast and act with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Bold, sharp, no-nonsense. The brand (marketing site) is loud and energetic — the admin should carry that identity without competing with the content. Brand shows up in the shell (sidebar, header, accents); content surfaces stay neutral and legible.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-gray: full gray-on-white with zero brand identity (current state)
- Over-decorated dashboards with gradient cards and hero metrics
- Tiny muted text everywhere — operators work fast, contrast matters

## Design Principles

1. **Shell carries the brand, content is neutral.** Sidebar and top bar reflect IMBA's bold identity; tables, forms, and data panels stay restrained and legible.
2. **Numbers first.** Metric values are the primary element in stat cards — larger, bolder, immediately scannable.
3. **Status must be unambiguous.** Green/amber/red with labels, never color alone.
4. **Earned familiarity.** Standard affordances for standard tasks. No invented patterns for filter dropdowns or modals.
5. **Density over decoration.** Tight rows, readable at a glance. No card grids for list data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High contrast text (≥4.5:1 body, ≥3:1 large). All interactive elements keyboard-reachable. Status never communicated by color alone.
